Process

How an engagement runs

01 Discovery

Two weeks fixed price: business flow, data, integrations and risk before production code.

02 Architecture

A written note you keep: system diagram, data model, failure modes, deployment, decisions to revisit.

03 Build in slices

Working software every week behind a demo link, so scope hardens against something you can use.

04 Harden

Tests, CI, security review, load checks, error handling, backups and the runbook.

05 Launch

Store release or deployment, monitoring and a handover session.

06 Operate

Optional maintenance window with response times and a monthly health report.

Engagement models

Fixed scope

Defined product and deadline. We scope, commit to a price and ship. Best for new products and rebuilds.

Team extension

Senior engineers who own a surface end to end, monthly, with a weekly demo.

Rescue and audit

Failing releases, security findings, no tests. We audit, stabilise and hand back a runbook.
